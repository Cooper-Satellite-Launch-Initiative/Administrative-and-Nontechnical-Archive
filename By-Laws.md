## Article 1: Name, Mission

### Section 1: Name
The official name of this organization is the Cooper Union Satellite Launch Initiative. This organization may also be referred to in shorthand as CUSLI or Cooper Cubesat.

### Section 2: Mission
The mission of CUSLI is to further the development of space related studies at the Cooper Union while also providing a safe, friendly, and supporitve enviroment to do so. Activites of CUSLI can include but are not limited to development of a Cubesat mission, hosting internal events, and collaborting with external organizations.

## Article 2: Membership
1. Membership will be granted to all interested persons who is a member of the Cooper Union
2. No member will be discrimated on the basis of race, religion, national origin, ethnicity, color, age, gender, gender identity, marital status, citizenship, sexual orientation, or disability
3. Members are encouraged to attend events hosted by CUSLI and become more involved to reach Ative member status
4. An Active Member will be defince as any persons who is:
    1. Registared on the JAC
    2. A contributor to any subsystem or club managment
    3. Proof they have attended at least one CUSLI meeting if at least two meeting have occured in that school year
    4. Alternative means to Active Memeber Status
        - Active enrollment in the VIP for Cubesat during that semester (See above course code)
        - **Or** Staff employed by the Cooper Union who supports the CUSLI in technical or organizational capacities
5. Active members will be granted the following rights
    1. Voting for board positions
    2. Nominating or self nominating for board positions

## Article 3: Board, Responsibilites
### Section 1: Minimum State of the Board
The CUSLI Board will consist of a minimum of three members
1. President
2. Chief Engineer
3. Treasurer

Additional board seats will be allocated to each subsystem, a secretary, and the professor of the VIP for which CUSLI work is being done.

### Section 2: Subsystem and Their Leads
A subsystem is defined as a project within the fabrication of the Cubesat which can be done independantly of the whole. Further detail:
1. To create a new subsystem a student must propose a new subsystem and be approved by two of the three 
    1. Professor 
    2. President 
    3. Chief engineer
2. A subsytem will be grant its own repository with in the Cooper Satellite Launch Initiative Github organzation which must be actively maintained
3. If a subsystem is not actively being worked on by a student it will be termed inactive
    1. An inactive subsystem will not have a lead and will not be represented on the board
    2. To reactivative an inactive subsytem only requires a student iniative and the approval of either the profesor, the president, or the chief engineer
4. A subsystem does not necassiate a lead inorder for progress to be made within that
5. Each active subsystem may have one person represent it on the board

Subsystem Lead is the person who actively working on the project and is expected to be highly knowledgable on the system. They will by default be on the board to represent their subsystem.
