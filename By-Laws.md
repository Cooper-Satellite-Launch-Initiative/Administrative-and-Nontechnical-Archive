## Article 1: Name, Mission

### Section 1: Name
The official name of this organization is the Cooper Union Satellite Launch Initiative. This organization may also be referred to in shorthand as CUSLI or Cooper Cubesat.

### Section 2: Mission
The mission of CUSLI is to further the development of space related studies at the Cooper Union while also providing a safe, friendly, and supporitve enviroment to do so. Activites of CUSLI can include but are not limited to development of a Cubesat mission, hosting internal events, and collaborting with external organizations.

## Article 2: Membership
1. Membership will be granted to all interested persons who is a member of the Cooper Union
2. No member will be discrimated on the basis of race, religion, national origin, ethnicity, color, age, gender, gender identity, marital status, citizenship, sexual orientation, or disability
3. Members are encouraged to attend events hosted by CUSLI and become more involved to reach Ative member status
4. An Active Member will be defince as any persons who is:
    1. Registared on the JAC
    2. A contributor to any subsystem or club managment
    3. Proof they have attended at least one CUSLI meeting if at least two meeting have occured in that school year
    4. Alternative means to Active Memeber Status
        - Active enrollment in the VIP for Cubesat during that semester (See below VIP code)
        - **Or** Staff employed by the Cooper Union who supports the CUSLI in technical or organizational capacities
5. Active members will be granted the following rights
    1. Voting for board positions
    2. Nominating or self nominating for board positions

## Article 3: Board, Responsibilites
### Section 1: Minimum State of the Board
The CUSLI Board will consist of a minimum of three members
1. President
2. Chief Engineer
3. Treasurer

Additional board seats will be allocated to each subsystem, additional roles ie secretary, and the professor of the VIP for which CUSLI work is being done.

### Section 2: Subsystem and Their Leads
A subsystem is defined as a project within the fabrication of the Cubesat which can be done independantly of the whole. Further detail:
1. To create a new subsystem a student must propose a new subsystem and be approved by two of the three 
    1. Professor 
    2. President 
    3. Chief engineer
2. A subsytem will be grant its own repository with in the Cooper Satellite Launch Initiative Github organzation which must be actively maintained
3. If a subsystem is not actively being worked on by a student it will be termed inactive
    1. An inactive subsystem will not have a lead and will not be represented on the board
    2. To reactivative an inactive subsytem only requires a student iniative and the approval of either the profesor, the president, or the chief engineer
4. A subsystem does not necassiate a lead inorder for progress to be made within that
5. Each active subsystem may have one person represent it on the board

Subsystem Lead is the person who actively working on the project and is expected to be highly knowledgable on the system. They will by default be on the board to represent their subsystem.

### Section 3 President, Chief Engineer, Treasurer, and Secretary
Duties of the President
1. Be the public voice of the Cooper Union Satellite Launch Initiative
2. Have the authority to enter contracts on behalf of CUSLI
3. Call and lead regular or special meetings of CUSLI
4. Settle disputes between members of CUSLI
5. Work with the Treasure on budget and grants
6. Maintain meeting minutes
7. Maintain the JAC and other forms of CUSLI publicity
8. Ensure compliance with all applicable rules


Duties of the Chief Engineer
1. Lead the overall technical project of CUSLI
2. Maintain strong communication between the subsystem leads
3. Ensure members are maintain upto date documentation 
4. Assumes the duties of the president in their absence
5. Ensure compliance with all applicable rules

Duties of the Treasurer
1. Request funding from the school via JAC and other interal grants of the Cooper Union
2. Making the necassary purchases on the behalf of the club
3. Write grant requests for external grants such as but not limited to New York Space Grant Consortium
4. Maintain a clear financial tracking method
5. Ensure compliance with all applicable rules

Duties of the Secretary (optional)
1. Maintain meeting minutes
2. Maintain JAC and other forms of CUSLI publicity
3. Advertise events hosted by CUSLI

### Section 4: Resignation and Removal of Board members
Resignation
1. Minimum one week notice must be given prior to a resignation of any board member
2. No additional reason or requirement 

Conditions for Removal: Failure to perform designated duties

If this criterion is deemed to have been met then a meeting with at least half of student board member present must reach a two-thirds majority in favor of removal.

## Article 4: Elections and Amenments
### Section 1: Election of board positions
1. Elections should ideally occur at least one month prior to the end of the Spring semester
2. Positions open to annual re-election
    1. President
    2. Treasurer
    3. Chief Engineer
    4. Any none subsystem board position should they be created
3. Subsystem leads will be open to election if the current lead wishes to step down or is graduating that spring
4. All interested candidates in any position must declare their interest in a role prior to the election to the board
    1. No person may hold more than two board positions at once
5. All present active members will vote in open elections via a ranked choice system

### Amending the By Laws
1. Any active member may propose an amendment to the Board
    1. Propsals are restrict such that they would not violate the current By Laws, Cooper Regulations, or any Local, State, or Federal law
2. All board members have one week to vote on the amendment
    1. Failure of a board member to vote will result in their vote being mark as abstain
3. For an amendement to pass it must have at least 51% of all board members vote in favor
